## Welcome to My Portfolio!

This site is inspired by the Xbox 360 NXE (New Xbox Experience) dashboard. I built it as a fun, nostalgic way to showcase my work as a software engineer.

### Features

- **Blade Navigation** - Swipe or use arrow keys to navigate between sections
- **Achievement System** - Unlock achievements as you explore!
- **Sound Effects** - Toggle audio for the full experience
- **Boot Sequence** - Watch the Xbox-style startup animation

### What's Next

I'm planning to add more features in future updates:
- 3D avatar display
- More achievements to unlock
- Additional projects

Thanks for visiting! Feel free to explore and unlock some achievements along the way.

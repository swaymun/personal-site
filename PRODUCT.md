# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Visitors learning about Saimun Shahee, browsing his interests and work, and playing with nostalgic handhelds on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose
A minimal personal text website with five interactive handheld interpretations of the same content and five original short games.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static Astro and TypeScript. Real content is reused from the prior site; unknown favorites and posts stay explicitly empty. No accounts, server, or background data collection. Notes and scores stay in the browser. Text content works without games or JavaScript. Local preview plus GitHub main pushes and automatic Vercel production deployment are authorized.

## Brand Commitments
White background, system typography, blue underlined links, centered top navigation: Home, GBA SP, PSP, iPod, 3DS, Switch. Nat.org and www.knarfeel.com are explicit references. Photographic product cutouts with calibrated clickable controls. Faithful period OS layouts and sourced interface assets. Cobalt SP, black PSP-3000, black/silver iPod touch 4 with iOS 6, midnight-purple original 3DS, Animal Crossing Switch. Device alone on a blank page. Build directly from references, no generated mockups.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing biography, employment, education, social links and project. Letterboxd favorites verified September 5, 2026: After Hours, Catch Me If You Can, Infernal Affairs, Rope.

## Product Principles
Content remains readable outside the handheld. Games are small but complete. Navigation never waits for audio. Load only what is used. Never invent personal rankings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard and touch operation, readable expanded view, text fallback, reduced motion, muted audio by default.
